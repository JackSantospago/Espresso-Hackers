# Knowledge base sources

The files in `assets/knowledge/` were written in plain language from the sources below (paraphrased, not copied). Each paragraph ends with `(Source: …)`, so the app can show where an answer came from. Researched and drafted on 3 Oct 2026. **Before relying on this in the field, have an agronomist or extension officer review it.** Chemical advice is kept general on purpose and always refers the farmer to an extension officer for products and doses.

## Known gaps

- English only. The LLM answers in the farmer's language, but retrieval works best when the question and passages share a language.
- Written for East African smallholders first. Coverage of temperate and irrigated farming is thin.
- The Phoma material comes mainly from Brazil, which matches the BRACOL training images.
